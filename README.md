# Privacy Policy for Gigasmart

Gigasmart ("we", "our", or "us") provides a mobile application that uses the device camera to scan device serial numbers.

## Information We Collect
The Gigasmart app may access the device **camera** to scan serial numbers.  
No images or videos are stored or shared by the app unless required for the core functionality of the application.

## How We Use the Camera
We use the device camera only to:
- Scan barcodes or QR codes
- Read device serial numbers

We do NOT:
- Capture personal photos
- Store or upload images to external servers (modify if your app does)
- Share camera data with third parties

- **Microphone:** (remove if not needed)
